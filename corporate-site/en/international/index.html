What we coordinate

Practical support around the medical visit

These are areas in which we can help coordinate. We confirm the hospital, timing, team and fees for each enquiry. We do not promise an appointment or a clinical outcome.

01 / BEFORE TRAVEL

Records and hospital process

We help organise your existing reports, scans and medication information, and clarify the questions you want to ask the hospital.

  • Communication in English or Chinese
  • Checking departments and booking routes
  • Hospital confirmation before travel
02 / IN CHINA

Visits and daily logistics

Once the hospital plan is confirmed, we can help coordinate routes, accompaniment, transport and accommodation needs. Your agreed service plan sets out what is included.

  • Visit schedule and directions
  • Accompaniment or interpreting needs
  • Transport, stay and food preferences
03 / AFTER THE VISIT

Results and next steps

We can help keep track of how to obtain reports and what the hospital has asked you to do next, including follow-up after you return home.

  • Report collection information
  • Follow-up schedule checks
  • Coordination within your consent

How it works

From your first question to a plan for what comes next

We check feasibility before you book travel. Hospitals and doctors remain responsible for medical decisions.

  1. 01

    Tell us your goal

    Share your preferred travel dates, location and main question. Please do not post medical records in public comments.

  2. 02

    Organise records

    With your consent, we collect only the information needed through an agreed secure channel.

  3. 03

    Check with the hospital

    We clarify the department, booking route, acceptance conditions and expected timing.

  4. 04

    Plan your trip

    Confirm service scope, fees and cancellation terms before arranging transport and accommodation.

  5. 05

    Follow through

    Keep track of report collection and the hospital's next steps.

Planning checklist

What to check before you travel

Use this list to plan your trip. Confirm current requirements with the hospital, insurer and relevant immigration authorities.

  • Write down the main question you want the hospital to address
  • Gather existing reports, scans, medication and allergy information
  • Confirm the department, appointment route and likely process
  • Check travel documents, entry rules, payment and insurance arrangements
  • Book travel after confirming the hospital plan, allowing time for tests or follow-up
